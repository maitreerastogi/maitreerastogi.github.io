Campus Connect

Your whole campus, in one place

The problem

Students depend on many WhatsApp groups, notice boards and social pages to find college information. Announcements get missed and old messages get buried in group chats.

The solution

One organised platform for verified notices, events, clubs, study materials, internships and lost-and-found, with separate access for faculty, clubs and administration.

Who it's for

College students, clubs and societies, faculty members, and college administration.

Why it can grow

Start in one college, gather feedback, then expand to many institutions. Revenue can come from college subscriptions, premium features, sponsored events and internship partnerships.
