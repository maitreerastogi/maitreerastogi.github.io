AGR · 01

AI Crop Problem Detector

Catch crop disease before it spreads

The problem

Farmers often spot crop disease or pests too late, once yield loss has already happened — and expert help isn't always nearby or affordable.

The solution

Photograph a leaf and an AI model flags likely diseases or pests, with treatment steps and an option to reach a local agri-expert.

Who it's for

Small and mid-scale farmers, agricultural cooperatives, and agri-input retailers.

Why it can grow

A recurring, season-after-season problem: free basic detection, paid expert reports, plus licensing deals with agri-input companies.

CAM · 02

Student Book Rental / Exchange

Stop paying full price for a book you'll use once

The problem

Students spend heavily on textbooks used for one semester, then the books sit unused with no easy way to rent or exchange them nearby.

The solution

A campus platform to list books for rent, sale, or exchange, with simple chat and a safe on-campus pickup point.

Who it's for

College and university students, starting on one campus and expanding to nearby institutions.

Why it can grow

Cost pressure repeats every semester, so students keep coming back — and it can expand into notes exchange or student-focused ads.

FIN · 03

Student Budget & Expense App

Money tools built for irregular student income

The problem

Students manage money for the first time with no planning experience — overspending early and losing track of where it goes.

The solution

A lightweight app for quick expense logging, spend breakdowns, low-balance alerts, and budget goals tied to the semester cycle.

Who it's for

College and university students with limited, irregular income, especially first-time hostel or PG residents.

Why it can grow

An underserved niche versus finance apps built for salaried adults — cheap to scale, with room for student discount or card partnerships.
